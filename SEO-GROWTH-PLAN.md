# SEO Growth Plan

**Updated:** 26 November 2026. The four-page November 25 production baseline remains documented in [SEO-REPORT.md](SEO-REPORT.md); the repository now contains seven indexable routes. The three new service routes await authorized publication and production verification.

## Before release

- Run the repository checks. Before release, complete browser and screenshot comparisons against the approved design baseline on the supported viewports; this environment could not perform them.
- Release only through the authorized GitHub Pages process. See [POST-LAUNCH-SEO.md](POST-LAUNCH-SEO.md) for exact commands, URLs and expected results. No release is authorized by this plan.
- Confirm product-specific availability, pricing/terms, platform listings and support arrangements before publishing those details. Company ownership and the Kounga Games brand relationship are established.
- Confirm existing homepage statistics, 24/7 support, consulting scope, and privacy/consent details with the business owner.

Visible-content opportunities are recommendations only and require explicit approval; see [SEO-CONTENT-RECOMMENDATIONS.md](SEO-CONTENT-RECOMMENDATIONS.md) for priority, benefit, impact, complexity and evidence requirements.

## First 30 days after release

- Verify HTTPS and redirects, all seven canonical routes, robots, sitemap, assets, analytics and contact form.
- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap, and inspect each route. Record the first available query/page impressions, clicks, indexing, canonical selection and field CWV baseline.
- Confirm GA4 `generate_lead` only records successful submissions and configure it as a conversion/key event if desired.
- Run mobile/desktop PageSpeed or Lighthouse checks and retain actual reports. Prioritize user-facing bottlenecks rather than chasing scores.
- Establish an initial Search Console and Bing baseline before considering page copy, titles, or page-target changes. Keep measured traffic and query data separate from qualitative SERP samples.
- Confirm Google Business Profile eligibility and details before changing or creating a profile.

## Days 31–60

- Use actual Search Console queries and sales questions to review all five service routes and the product directory.
- The IT consulting, branding/design and IT support routes have been added using the existing approved service descriptions. Confirm target customers, deliverables, exclusions, engagement models and service boundaries before expanding visible content or making response-time/availability claims.
- Gather client permission and verifiable project facts before adding case studies, client names, logos, outcomes or testimonials.
- Confirm current product details and support arrangements before considering product detail pages or adding more product-specific structured data. The six current product entities are already connected to their confirmed legal company in the directory markup.

The site remains static. The existing GitHub Actions workflow runs `python scripts/check_seo.py` on pull requests and pushes to `main`; the checker discovers all current `index.html` routes rather than relying on a fixed page count.

## Days 61–90 and recurring work

- Compare relevant non-branded Auckland/NZ query coverage, qualified enquiries and engagement against the verified baseline. Do not set ranking promises or traffic targets without evidence.
- Review indexing, broken links, sitemap/canonical coverage, image payloads, real-user CWV, form success and claim accuracy monthly.
- Quarterly, revisit service priorities, approved case studies, local business information, product availability and privacy/consent practices.
- Update the audit with observed Search Console, Bing, GA4 and PageSpeed data; keep lab and field measurements separate.

## Success measures

Track qualified non-branded impressions and clicks, indexed priority URLs, selected canonical URLs, qualified successful form submissions, and mobile/desktop field CWV when available. Search visibility and AI citations are not guaranteed.
